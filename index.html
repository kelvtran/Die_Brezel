ABOUT US

Tucked away in the heart of Köln is your new favorite spot for fresh, authentic German pretzels and artisanal coffee.
Enjoy our light filled rooms, beautiful garden, cozy atmosphere, and friendly service.
Whether you're grabbing a quick bite or looking for a place to relax, Die Brezel is the perfect destination for pretzel lovers and coffee enthusiasts alike.
